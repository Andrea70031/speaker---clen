Un kit completo per gli speaker.

Sonic MD riunisce pulizia guidata, test comparativi e modalità dedicate a contaminanti comuni come acqua, polvere e sabbia.

Sonic MD combines guided cleaning, comparative tests and dedicated modes for common contaminants such as water, dust and sand.

Smart Scan

Water Rescue
